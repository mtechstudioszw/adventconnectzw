Paste-ready copy for the rebrand. **This is an update to the existing Play
listing**, not a new app. Do not create a new application, do not change the
application ID (`io.supabase.adventconnectzw.advent_connect_zw`), and do not
change the signing key.

## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Adventist Super App connects Seventh-day Adventists around the globe — one
app for your church, your community and your walk with God.

Wherever you are in the world, find your people.

COMMUNITY FEED
Share what God is doing in your life. Post photos, updates and stories,
```

## What's new (500 char max) — v1.4.0

Play truncates this in the store to about two lines, so the headline comes
first and detail follows.

```
The app is now worldwide.

Tell us your country once and everything follows: churches, events,
marketplace and jobs all show your country first, with worldwide one tap
away. Sell in your own currency. WhatsApp buttons now dial correctly
wherever you are.

Can't find your church? Add it, and it goes on the map for everyone.

Also: far fewer notifications, a faster church search, and a lot of small
fixes.
```

> 383 characters. If a shorter cut is needed, keep the first line and the
> "Add it" line — those are the two that matter to a new member abroad.

### Why it leads with "worldwide" and not a feature list

The one thing a returning member needs to know is that the app is no longer
Zimbabwe-shaped. "Far fewer notifications" is deliberately last and
deliberately vague — the honest version ("we were sending you 23 a day") is
not something to put in a store listing, but the improvement is real and
worth signalling.

## Store settings to check

- **Category:** Lifestyle (or Social) — unchanged.
- **Countries/regions:** currently may be Zimbabwe-only. To go global this
  must be expanded to all countries in Play Console → Production →
  Countries/regions. **This is the single setting that actually makes the
  app available worldwide** — the rebrand alone does not.
- **Store listing translations:** consider adding at least Portuguese,
  Spanish and French, the largest Adventist language groups after English.
- **In-app product titles:** the Premium subscription is titled "Advent
  Connect Premium" in Play Console. Rename the *display title* only — never
  the product ID, or existing subscribers break.
