## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Adventist Super App connects Seventh-day Adventists around the globe — one
app for your church, your community and your walk with God.

Wherever you are in the world, find your people.

COMMUNITY FEED
Share what God is doing in your life. Post photos, updates and stories,
react and comment, and keep up with members and churches you follow.

CHURCHES
Search Adventist congregations by name or city, see service times, pastors
and directions, and follow your home church so its announcements and events
land in your feed. Zimbabwe is mapped in full, and more countries are being
added over time.

PRAYER
Share prayer requests with the worldwide Adventist community and pray for
others. Join prayer circles and mark answered prayers.

MESSAGING
One-to-one and group chats, voice notes, photo sharing, and church groups
for announcements.

LIBRARY — WORKS OFFLINE
The full Bible, Sabbath School lessons, a hymnal of nearly 1,000 hymns, and
Ellen G. White books. Download once and read anywhere, with no data.

SABBATH
Sundown times, a Sabbath countdown, and a quieter app on the seventh day.

WATCH
Adventist sermons, music and short videos in one place.

BIBLE QUIZ
Test your Bible knowledge solo or head-to-head against members worldwide,
with weekly leaderboards.

EVENTS
Discover camp meetings, concerts, weeks of prayer and local gatherings, and
RSVP so you never miss one.

MARKETPLACE & JOBS
Buy and sell within the Adventist community, and find opportunities shared
by members. Listings show their real location so you always know who you
are dealing with.

FREE
Every feature is free. An optional Premium subscription removes ads and
supports the app's running costs.

---

Adventist Super App is an independent app built to serve the Adventist
community. It is not an official Seventh-day Adventist Church product, and
it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the General
Conference, any division, union or local conference.

Questions or feedback: adventconnectzw@gmail.com
```

## Store settings to check

- **Category:** Lifestyle (or Social) — unchanged.
- **Countries/regions:** currently may be Zimbabwe-only. To go global this
  must be expanded to all countries in Play Console → Production →
  Countries/regions. **This is the single setting that actually makes the
  app available worldwide** — the rebrand alone does not.
- **Store listing translations:** consider adding at least Portuguese,
  Spanish and French, the largest Adventist language groups after English.
- **In-app product titles:** the Premium subscription is titled "Advent
  Connect Premium" in Play Console. Rename the *display title* only — never
  the product ID, or existing subscribers break.

- "Official SDA app" / "Official Seventh-day Adventist app"
- Church endorsement or affiliation
- That the directory contains every Adventist church worldwide
