About

A community-built platform for Adventist Zimbabwe.

What is Advent Connect ZW?

Advent Connect ZW is a community platform for the Seventh-day Adventist community in Zimbabwe. It brings the things that used to live in scattered WhatsApp groups, paper bulletins, and printed directories into one place that's actually built for the way the community already operates.

What's inside

  • Churches: a searchable directory of every SDA church across Zimbabwe.
  • Events: camp meetings, week-of-prayer, youth gatherings, conferences — discover, RSVP, share.
  • Prayer wall: share requests, pray together, encourage one another.
  • Marketplace: Adventist-values trading between members. Self-serve with a strict Code of Conduct.
  • Advent Chat: private, realtime messaging with read receipts.
  • Advent News: editorial coverage of what's trending in the community.
