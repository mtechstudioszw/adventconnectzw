Community Guidelines

Last updated: 27 May 2026

Advent Connect ZW exists to serve the Seventh-day Adventist community in Zimbabwe. These guidelines protect that purpose — whether you're posting a prayer request, RSVPing to an event, chatting one-on-one, or selling something in the marketplace.

1. Be honest

  • Use your real name and a real profile photo.
  • Don't impersonate other members, churches, or conference bodies.
  • Marketplace photos must show the actual item; descriptions and prices must be accurate.

2. Respect the Sabbath

From Friday sundown to Saturday sundown, treat the platform as a space for worship and rest. No high-pressure sales, no spam pings, no deliveries pushed during the Sabbath.

3. Adventist values in content

Anything you post represents the community. Don't promote what contradicts Adventist beliefs and practices, including:

  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, recreational drugs.
  • Unclean foods (per Leviticus 11), e.g. pork, shellfish.
  • Gambling, lotteries, betting.
  • Immodest clothing, intimate apparel, jewelry framed as adornment.
  • Occult, horoscope, divination, or witchcraft items.
  • Violent, sexually explicit, or graphic media.
  • Counterfeit, pirated, or stolen goods.
  • Anything illegal under Zimbabwean law.

4. Be kind

  • No harassment, threats, insults, or hate speech.
  • No targeting of specific people or churches with public criticism — handle disputes privately or with leadership.
  • Disagree respectfully. We are family, not enemies.

5. Keep prayer prayer

The Prayer wall is sacred space. Don't use it for political soapboxing, marketing, doctrinal arguments, or anonymous attacks on named individuals. Pray with people, don't perform piety.

6. Marketplace honesty

  • Real photos. Honest descriptions. Fair prices.
  • No fake reviews, no manufactured "social proof".
  • Don't ghost buyers — if you can't fulfill, say so and refund.
  • Disputes happen offline. Don't drag fellow members into public chats over a sale gone wrong — message us instead.

7. Privacy

  • Don't share other members' phone numbers, addresses, or photos without consent.
  • Don't screenshot private chats to publicly shame people.
  • Don't post members' medical / financial / family-crisis details without permission.

8. Reporting

Tap the menu (⋯) on any post, prayer, message, or seller to report a violation. You can also email tanatswamichaelmikuwa@gmail.com or WhatsApp +263 778 092 494 directly.

9. Enforcement

First-time violations get a warning + content removal. Repeated or serious violations (scams, harassment, illegal content, anything in the section 3 "not allowed" list) result in permanent ban with no appeal — your storefront is taken down, your products removed, your account closed.

10. We're a community, not a content platform

Every member here is a brother or sister in Christ. Behave accordingly. "Whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them." (Matthew 7:12)
