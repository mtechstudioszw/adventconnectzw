Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026

1. Who we are

Advent Connect ZW (the "App") is operated by Tanatswa Michael Mikuwa, trading as MyTech Studios Zw, based in Zimbabwe. Contact: tanatswamichaelmikuwa@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you use the App we may collect:

  • Account data: email, full name, date of birth, profile photo, cover photo, bio, home church, username.
  • Content you create: posts, prayer requests, comments, events, marketplace listings, messages, voice notes, stories.
  • Device data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token so we can send you push notifications you opted into.
  • Location data: latitude/longitude only when you tap "Near me" on the Churches tab, and only for that request. We do not track you in the background.
  • Usage diagnostics: crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics. We do not link these to your identity beyond what Crashlytics needs to deduplicate crashes.

3. What we DON'T collect

  • Your contacts list.
  • Your photos beyond the ones you explicitly upload.
  • Background location.
  • Microphone or camera input unless you tap to record a voice note or take a photo.
  • Financial details — donations go through PayNow / EcoCash externally; we never see your card or wallet.

4. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Show your content to other members where you intended (public, friends-only, church-only, or anonymous).
  • Send you the notifications you opted into in Settings.
  • Find your nearest churches and events.
  • Investigate abuse and remove violating content.

We do not sell your data to advertisers or any third party. We do not run ads inside the App.

5. Who can see your content

Visibility is determined by you when you post:

  • Public posts, events, prayers, marketplace listings, and Advent News are visible to all signed-in members.
  • Friends-only posts are visible only to your accepted friends.
  • Church-only prayers are visible to fellow members of your home church.
  • Anonymous prayers hide your name from everyone.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient.

6. Where we store data

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL, hosted in their Africa (Cape Town) region), Firebase (push + crash reporting, Google Cloud), and on your device via encrypted secure storage (auth tokens) and local cache (recent messages / feed).

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account data persists until you delete your account.
  • Stories auto-expire after 24 hours.
  • Messages persist until you or the other participant deletes them.
  • Crash diagnostics are retained per Firebase Crashlytics defaults (90 days).

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile row, your content, and your auth user. Some data may persist briefly in backups until they age out (typically within 30 days).

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. The signup flow includes an age gate.

10. Security

Auth tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences / iOS Keychain). All data in transit uses HTTPS. Row-level security policies on the database enforce that you can only read/write data you're allowed to.

11. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Edit or remove individual posts, prayers, messages, events.
  • Turn off any category of push notification from Settings.
  • Delete your account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing tanatswamichaelmikuwa@gmail.com.

12. Changes

We'll update this page (and the "Last updated" date above) when our practices change. Material changes will be surfaced inside the App.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: tanatswamichaelmikuwa@gmail.com or WhatsApp +263 778 092 494.
