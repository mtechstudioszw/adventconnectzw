# Advent Connect ZW — How to use the app

A plain guide to everything in the app, written for members rather than
developers. If you only read one section, read **Getting started**.

---

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** with your email address, name and date of birth. You
   must be 16 or older.
2. **Set your home church.** This is the single most useful thing you can do —
   it shapes which churches, events and people the app shows you.
3. **Add a photo and a short bio.** People are far more likely to accept a
   friend request from a profile that looks like a real person.

Your sign-in details are stored securely on your device, so you stay signed in
between visits.

---

## The five tabs

Along the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your feed, plus quick access to everything else |
| **Watch** | Live TV, sermons and video |
| **Chat** | Messages, friends and groups |
| **Marketplace** | Buying and selling within the community |
| **Profile** | You — your posts, friends and settings |

---

## Home

The feed shows posts from people and churches you follow, newest and most
relevant first.

**Posting.** Tap the composer at the top. You can add photos and choose who
sees it:

- **Public** — every signed-in member
- **Friends only** — only people you've accepted as friends

**Stories** run along the top and disappear after 24 hours.

**Today card.** A daily devotional, plus the day's Sabbath School lesson, a
hymn and a suggested book. The hymn alternates between Shona and English day by
day, so both languages come around equally.

**Library shortcuts.** A row of pills under the Today card: Bible, Sabbath,
Hymnal, **Quiz**, Music and EGW. This is the quickest way into any of them.

**Sabbath.** From Friday sundown to Saturday sundown the header changes — a
warm sundown wash, a gold line, and the greeting *Sabata rakanaka*. You can
turn the Sabbath countdown on or off in Settings.

---

## Watch

Live TV channels, sermons and recorded events. Tap any video to play it; it
continues in a small player while you browse. Some channels are live and
labelled as such.

---

## Chat

**Advent Chat** is private messaging between members.

- **Search** — the field at the top searches people, groups and message text
- **Find people** — in the ⋮ menu, for finding members you aren't friends with yet
- **Notes to self** — a private space for your own notes
- **Starred messages** — messages you've saved
- **Chat privacy** — control who is allowed to message you

**Reporting.** Press and hold any message to report it.

**Please note:** messages are **not end-to-end encrypted**. They're stored on
our servers and can be accessed by administrators when a report is made, or
where safety or the law requires it. Church admins cannot read your private
chats. Don't send anything you wouldn't want stored on a server.

**Blocking.** Blocking someone stops them messaging you, and hides their posts,
stories and prayers from you — in both directions.

---

## Marketplace

Browse items listed by other members, or sell your own.

Your **cart is on your device**, and checkout sends your order **directly to
each seller on WhatsApp** — one message per seller. The app doesn't process
payments and isn't part of the sale.

**Staying safe:**
- Meet in a public place, during the day
- Inspect the item before paying
- Never pay in advance for something you haven't seen
- Never share a banking PIN or one-time password

To sell, open **Seller mode** from the Marketplace and add a product.

## Library

Reached from the pills on Home, or the Library screen itself.

**Bible.** Multiple translations including Shona, plus Hebrew and Greek. One
translation has narrated audio. Your place is remembered.

**Sabbath School.** The current quarter's lessons, day by day, with a
*Continue reading* card that picks up where you stopped. Defaults to English;
around 90 languages are available from the language picker, and your choice is
remembered. Lessons are saved for offline reading.

**Hymnal.** 995 hymns in Shona and English, bundled with the app — they work
with no connection at all.

**EGW.** 61 Ellen G. White books, including the full Conflict of the Ages
series, all nine *Testimonies for the Church* and three *Selected Messages*.
Your reading position is saved per book.

> **Reading offline:** open a book's ⋮ menu and choose **Read offline**. The
> book is kept on your phone so you can read it with no data. Choose **Remove
> download** to free the space. This is different from *Save a copy*, which
> hands a copy to your phone's files or share sheet.

**Music.** Worship music and hymn recordings. Tap the download icon to keep a
track offline. Playback continues while you use other parts of the app.

## Quiz

A Bible quiz, reached from the **Quiz** pill on Home or from the Library.

- **Daily Challenge** — the same set of questions for everyone that day
- **Quick Play**, **Survival**, **Speed Round**, **Fix Your Mistakes**
- **Challenge a friend** and **live matches** against other members
- A weekly leaderboard that resets Sunday

Sound effects and background music are **separate switches** — turning off the
effects leaves the music playing, and vice versa.

---

## Prayer

Share a prayer request, or pray for someone else's. Requests can be **public**,
**church-only** or **anonymous** (your name is hidden from everyone). When you
pray for a request the person is told how many have prayed — never who.

Please treat prayer requests as confidential. Don't screenshot them or repost
them outside the app.

---

## Churches

A directory of Seventh-day Adventist congregations across Zimbabwe — around
2,600 of them. Search by name or city, or sort by nearest if you allow location
access.

**Following** a church puts its announcements and events in your feed.

**Claiming a church.** If you lead a congregation, open it and tap the claim
link. Claims are reviewed before approval, and a church can only be claimed by
one person. You need a connection to apply, so the app can check the church
isn't already claimed.

---

## Events, Jobs and News

- **Events** — church events and camp meetings; RSVP so the organiser knows
- **Jobs** — job listings posted by members
- **Advent News** — editorial coverage from the wider Adventist community

---

## Profile and Settings

Your profile holds your posts, photos, friends, ministry and spiritual gifts.

**Settings** covers:

- Notifications — pick exactly which alerts you receive
- Sound and haptics — including the quiz's separate music and effects switches
- Theme
- Chat privacy — who may message you
- Blocked members — review and unblock
- Sabbath countdown — on or off
- **Delete account** — permanent. You'll be asked to choose a reason first.

---

## Premium

An optional subscription that removes ads and unlocks extra features. Manage or
cancel it any time through Google Play — the app never handles your card.
