Community Guidelines

Keeping our community safe, kind, and Christ-centred.

Advent Connect ZW is a space for the Seventh-day Adventist community in Zimbabwe to connect, encourage one another, and share. These guidelines keep it welcoming for everyone. Breaking them may lead to content removal or account suspension.

Be respectful

  • Treat others with kindness, even in disagreement.
  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate speech toward any person or group.

Keep it appropriate

  • No sexually explicit, violent, or graphic content.
  • No content that promotes self-harm, illegal activity, or dangerous behaviour.

Be honest

  • No impersonation, scams, or deceptive listings.
  • No spam, repetitive posting, or misleading information.

Respect privacy & rights

  • Don't share other people's private information without consent.
  • Only post content you have the right to share.

Marketplace & jobs

  • List only genuine items and real opportunities.
  • Meet in safe, public places and inspect items before paying. The app is not a party to transactions between users.

Reporting

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, use the in-app report option. Our team reviews reports and may remove content or suspend accounts.
