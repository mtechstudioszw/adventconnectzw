Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Advent Connect ZW ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. The app is developed and operated by Tanatswa Michael Mikuwa (MyTech Studios Zw), an independent developer serving the Seventh-day Adventist community in Zimbabwe. The app is not affiliated with the General Conference or any local conference.

By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this policy. You must be at least 16 years old to use the app.

1. Information we collect

You provide directly

  • Account details: name, email address, date of birth (used only to verify you meet the minimum age), and a password (stored securely hashed — we never see it in plain text).
  • Profile information: profile photo, cover photo, bio, optional username, home church, and your province/city.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, prayer requests, events, marketplace listings, job posts, stories, and chat messages.
  • Support contact: any feedback or reports you submit.

Collected automatically

  • Device & push token: a notification token so we can deliver push notifications, plus app version and platform.
  • Location (optional): if you grant permission, your approximate or precise location is used only to sort and show nearby churches. You can deny or revoke this at any time in your device settings — the app still works without it.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and limited analytics to keep the app stable and improve it.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To show you community content, churches, events, prayers, the marketplace, and jobs.
  • To deliver messages and notifications you opt into.
  • To verify minimum age and keep the community safe (moderation of reported content, and suspension of accounts that break the guidelines).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

3. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers ("processors") that handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (hosted in the Africa / Cape Town region).
  • Google Firebase — push notifications, crash reporting, and limited analytics.
  • Google Sign-In (and Sign in with Apple on iOS) — if you choose to sign in with them.

Content you post publicly in the app (e.g. posts, listings, prayers) is visible to other users by design. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time — see our Account & Data Deletion page. When you delete your account, your profile and personal data are removed from our systems. Some content may be retained where required for legal or safety reasons.

5. Security

Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure storage. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to backend data is restricted by row-level security so users can only reach data they're entitled to.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your rights

You can access and update most of your information in the app, and you can delete your account. For any other request regarding your data, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated in the app.
